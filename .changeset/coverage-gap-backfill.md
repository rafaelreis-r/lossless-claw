Backfill transcript coverage gaps that a tail-only overlap used to hide. Live-turn ingestion persists recent turns directly into the DB, so a conversation that lost its bootstrap checkpoint (or holds an all-zero placeholder) ends each turn with a DB tail equal to the transcript tail while hundreds of middle messages were never imported; the reconcile tail comparison then reported in-sync with 0 imports and the checkpoint refreshed to EOF, making the missing middle permanently unreachable through append-only reads. The afterTurn checkpoint-missing and placeholder-checkpoint lanes now detect that shape — runtime session match, bootstrapped conversation, zero summaries, persisted rows covering less than half of the transcript with more than the cap floor missing — and import the gap with identity-occurrence accounting so already-persisted rows are never duplicated, before the checkpoint is allowed to advance. Healthy conversations whose DB already covers the transcript are unaffected.
